v0.1 “Génesis”

Defend the box. Attack from it. Defiende tu máquina. Ataca desde ella.

Linux-C.O.S is one security distribution for attackers (red team) and defenders (blue team): Kali's and Parrot's tools installed natively, BlackArch's tools in a container, real defensive tooling, a local AI copilot — and an operating system that is hardened from the first boot. Linux-C.O.S es una sola distribución de seguridad para quien ataca (red team) y para quien defiende (blue team): las herramientas de Kali y Parrot instaladas de forma nativa, las de BlackArch en un contenedor, herramientas defensivas de verdad, un copiloto de IA local — y un sistema operativo protegido desde el primer arranque.

AI copilotCopiloto de IA

A local AI for offense and defenseUna IA local para ofensiva y defensa

cos-ai runs a free, open-weight model on your own machine — no cloud, no API key. It explains tool output, suggests the next step for an authorized engagement, and writes blue-team detections. It assists; you stay in control.cos-ai ejecuta un modelo abierto y gratuito en tu propia máquina — sin nube, sin clave de API. Explica la salida de las herramientas, sugiere el siguiente paso en un trabajo autorizado y escribe detecciones blue-team. Asiste; tú mantienes el control.

kali@linux-cos: ~
kali@linux-cos:~$ nmap -sV 10.0.0.5 | cos-ai explain
ai > 10.0.0.5 exposes SSH (OpenSSH 8.2) and an outdated Apache 2.4.29
      on 80. The Apache build is the thing to look at next; check the
      version against known CVEs before anything else.
kali@linux-cos:~$ cos-ai blueteam "sigma rule for kerberoasting"
ai > # writes a Sigma rule mapped to ATT&CK T1558.003 ...
kali@linux-cos:~$ cos-ai setup   # installs Ollama + a model, once
